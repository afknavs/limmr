Calm by default. A glow where AI speaks.

Neutral surfaces and one blue accent carry the app. The multicolour edge glow appears only on things an AI wrote for you, so you always know what's yours and what's suggested. Every circle has its own colour and symbol, and that pair follows it everywhere: tiles, tasks, the people map.

Rules
1. Search first, then what needs you, then everything else.
2. No empty boxes. A section with nothing to show is hidden.
3. Text over images only on hover; a soft scrim makes it readable.
4. Images load as small cached thumbnails first, never block the page.
5. One card style. Shadows only on hover and floating panels.
6. Glow means AI. Blue means action. Amber means it waits on you.
