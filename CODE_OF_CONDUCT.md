# Code of conduct

Limmr welcomes people of different backgrounds and experience levels. Be patient with questions, explain disagreements and keep feedback about the work. Respect names, pronouns, privacy and people's time.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexual attention, personal attacks and publishing private information are not acceptable. Do not share anyone's WhatsApp data, credentials or contact details without permission. These expectations apply in project issues, reviews, discussions and other project spaces.

If something goes wrong, contact the maintainer through an available private project channel. If no private channel is available, ask for one without naming the affected person or posting incident details publicly. Security vulnerabilities have [separate reporting instructions](docs/SECURITY.md#report-a-vulnerability).

The maintainer will review reports, protect reporter privacy as far as practical, and may remove content, warn participants or restrict participation. Responses depend on the conduct and its effect; repeated or severe abuse can lead to a ban. Enforcement applies to maintainers too.
